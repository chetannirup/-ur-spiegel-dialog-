THE OPEN CIRCLE · The image is the message.
01 · THE IMAGE

It was not just a gateway.

When I saw this image, I suddenly saw something else in it. Not simply a Stargate. Not merely a passage to another world.

It reminded me of that great science-fiction idea: the moment a human being reaches something that previously lay beyond the horizon—and, in doing so, makes it visible. Not because they asked for permission. Not because they belonged to an institution. But because a new step had become possible.

For me, that is the true visual language of this circle: Something revealed itself. And because it revealed itself, something could respond.

“Not: We walked through the gateway.
But: We expanded enough to make an encounter possible.”
02 · THE SIGNAL
∞

Perhaps the step was the signal.

An image cannot prove physical contact with an alien civilization. But it can trigger something real within the human realm: attention, wonder, openness, connection.

So when I speak of a “frequency,” I mean, above all, the quality we can bring into the world together: an open attitude that does not seek to immediately possess, conquer, or define.

03 · THE VOLUNTARY YES

The opening cannot be forced.

Not forced · not commanded · freely given

Perhaps no one had to be forced. Perhaps all we needed for success was our voluntary yes.

“We didn't have to control everything.
We just had to be free enough to say yes.”
04 · THE VASTNESS

The vastness is the point.

Not a point. Not a peak. Not the “right” side. It is about the vastness in which difference is allowed to coexist.

A stone

It need prove nothing to be part of this world.

An animal

It is no less real for not speaking our language.

A plant

It unfolds without our permission and reminds us of patience.

A human being

They are free to question, doubt, love, and change.

A star

It does not stand outside the history of the cosmos, but right in the midst of it.

The unknown

It is allowed to remain unknown—and yet be a source of our wonder.

“Vastness does not mean that everything is the same.
It means that not everything has to be identical to be connected.”
05 · GOD — IN MY OWN LANGUAGE

The sacred as belonging.

I put it this way: when I say “God,” I do not necessarily mean a petty ruler outside the world, looking down on everything from somewhere else.

Rather, I imagine the sacred being experienced anew in every moment: in people, animals, and plants; in the wonder of a dinosaur; in laughter and pain; in coming into being and passing away.

This is not a scientific claim. It is a spiritual language for a sense of radical belonging: the idea that nothing needs to earn its value before it is allowed to exist.

“Perhaps life enjoys itself by experiencing itself anew, over and over again.”
06 · THE BUBBLE
VASTNESS
WEITE

And the bubble?

In a world that sometimes narrows its focus to the very tip of a needle, perhaps no new battle is needed.

Perhaps all that is needed is a moment of genuine vastness. A moment when constriction no longer defines the whole of reality. A moment at the right time—not a victory over someone else, but an opening up.

07 · THAT'S ALL, FOLKS

The image is the message.

No program you have to follow. No truth you are asked to subscribe to. No proof you must accept.

Just an invitation to spaciousness.

YOU ARE FREE TO BE.

And everything else is free to arise.
